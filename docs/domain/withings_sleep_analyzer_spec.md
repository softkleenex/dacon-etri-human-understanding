# Withings Sleep Analyzer Technical & Biological Specifications

This document outlines the operational mechanisms, biological measurement parameters, and score calculation logic of the **Withings Sleep Analyzer** [2], serving as a domain knowledge anchor for the DACON ETRI Human Daily Understanding challenge.

---

## 1. Device Overview & Sensing Technology
The Withings Sleep Analyzer is an under-the-mattress pneumatic tracking mat. Unlike wearables, it captures physiological signals passively and continuously throughout the night without direct body contact.

- **Sensor Type**: High-sensitivity pneumatic sensor (ballistocardiography).
- **Physical Placed Location**: Placed under the mattress, aligned at chest level.
- **Physical Signals Measured**:
  - **Ballistocardiography (BCG)**: Mechanical forces generated by the heart pumping blood, used to derive instantaneous heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV).
  - **Respiration**: Respiratory movements and chest wall displacement to measure breathing rate, depth, and respiratory pauses (apnea).
  - **Body Motion**: Gross body movements to evaluate sleep cycles and arousals.

---

## 2. Sleep Cycles & Stage Classification
The mat combines respiratory frequency, heart rate fluctuations, and physical movement to segment sleep into three core cycles:

1. **Light Sleep (N1 & N2 Stages)**:
   - *Domain Context*: Transition state. Easy to wake from. Breathing and heart rate start to slow.
   - *Model Signal*: Moderate physical movements, slight variability in respiration.
2. **Deep Sleep (N3 Stage / Slow Wave Sleep)**:
   - *Domain Context*: The physical restoration phase. Growth hormones are released, muscles relax, and tissue repair occurs.
   - *Model Signal*: High heart rate regularity, low respiratory rate variability, near-zero physical body movement.
3. **REM (Rapid Eye Movement / Dream Sleep)**:
   - *Domain Context*: Brain activity increases, dreaming occurs, critical for cognitive sorting, memory consolidation, and emotional regulation.
   - *Model Signal*: Irregular heart rate, rapid irregular breathing, but absolute skeletal muscle atonia (physical paralysis).

## 3. Sleep Score Architecture (Out of 100)
The Withings Sleep Score is computed as a weighted aggregate of six key sub-metrics:

### A. Duration (수면 시간)
- **Definition**: The total net sleep time.
- **Goal**: Score increases linearly until reaching the age-appropriate threshold (e.g., 7–9 hours for adults).

### B. Depth (수면 깊이)
- **Definition**: The proportion of restorative sleep (Deep Sleep + REM Sleep) relative to total sleep duration.
- **Goal**: Optimal recovery is achieved when Deep + REM sleep comprises a significant percentage of the night.

### C. Regularity (수면 규칙성)
- **Definition**: The day-to-day consistency of sleep onset (bedtime) and wake times.
- **Goal**: High score represents low circadian variance. Chronobiology indicates that high regularity stabilizes biological rhythms and positive subjective mood.

### D. Interruptions (수면 중 각성)
- **Definition**: Total time spent awake or out-of-bed after initially falling asleep.
- **Goal**: Minimizing wake sequences improves continuity.

### E. Time to Sleep / Sleep Onset Latency (SOL)
- **Definition**: The time elapsed between going to bed and falling asleep.
- **Goal**: Falling asleep too fast (under 5 mins) can indicate severe sleep deprivation, while taking too long (over 30 mins) indicates insomnia/onset delay. Optimum is around 15–20 minutes.

### F. Time to Get Up (기상 후 침대 지연 시간)
- **Definition**: The duration between waking up and physically getting out of bed.
- **Goal**: Minimizing prolonged lying awake post-wake.

## 4. Advanced Physiological Biomarkers
- **Overnight Mean Heart Rate**: A standard biomarker of cardiovascular strain. High mean nocturnal heart rate suggests high stress, incomplete recovery, or elevated sympathetic drive.
- **Overnight Heart Rate Variability (HRV)**: Measured continuously. A high HRV indicates robust parasympathetic activation (vagal tone) and excellent recovery, while a suppressed HRV is a major predictor of stress, fatigue, and potential subjective depression.
- **Breathing Disturbances & Sleep Apnea Detection**: Continuous respiration tracing to detect temporary cessations in breathing, calculated as an Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI) equivalent.
